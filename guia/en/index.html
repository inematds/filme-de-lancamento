In one sentence

You have a good business, but no video to show it. Film by Conversation is a free, open kit for making a launch film of about 30 seconds without editing software, by talking with Claude Code, an AI that creates files on your computer. It does not guarantee sales, followers, customers, or income. It helps you make and review the film. Business results depend on the product, who sees it, and what you do next.

How to start

How to start

The AI reads the kit, explains the steps, and asks for your approval before changing anything.

1

Open the kit folder in Claude

On Windows or Mac, install the Claude app and sign in to your account. Download the kit from GitHub using the Code button, then Download ZIP, and unzip it. In the app, open the Code tab, choose Local, and use Open folder… to select the folder. You do not need the terminal. This is the official route, but it has not been tested with the kit yet. On Linux, where the kit was tested, the full setup guide shows how to open it.

2

Paste the prompt

It helps the AI learn about the kit and ask about your business. To send photos and the logo, drag the files into the conversation or say where they are, and ask the AI to organize them. The kit's prompts are written in Portuguese, and the AI can walk you through them in English.

3

Say yes to what makes sense

The AI guides you one step at a time. To continue, just ask it to "follow the file prompts/P1", and so on, without opening any files. You approve the story, the images, and every change.

Questions

Common questions

What people often ask before starting.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not to direct the film: you talk with the AI. The AI handles the technical part, but you need Claude Code installed and must approve what it does.

Do I need to know how to edit video?

No. You watch, say what you want to change, and point to the second.

What does it cost, and what do I need?

The kit is free, and the film is generated on your own computer. You need a paid Claude plan. The official pricing page says all paid plans include Claude Code, so any of them will work to get started.

The AI may need to install free programs, with your approval first: Node.js runs the program that puts the film together, FFmpeg combines image and sound, Chrome draws each frame, and Python measures the music if you use any.

Will it work on my computer?

The kit was tested on Linux. Windows and Mac have official Claude installation options, but the kit has not been tested on them yet. Minimum memory and disk requirements have not been measured. On these systems, first test with the example film before customizing: ask the AI to generate it without changing anything.

Can I use the example's music for my business?

No. It is for non-commercial use only. Your film can have no music, with effects only. If you want music, one example is Incompetech: on 18/11/2026, the site listed a license that allows commercial use with credit to the author. Read the license before using it.

Can I make a film for someone else's brand?

As a private sample, marked unofficial, using the official logo without changing it. Do not use other people's photos without permission or show customer or employee faces. If the business has no published brand guidelines, use the logo from its own website or profile, and ask permission before any public use.

Does this guarantee customers?

No. The kit helps you show what you have more clearly.

Which of your customer's problems deserves a 30-second film? The kit is free: download it in How to start.
